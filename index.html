Common questions

BingoPlus Slot FAQ

What is BingoPlus Slot?

BingoPlus Slot refers to slot-related game information and content associated with the BingoPlus brand. Use the linked website to check current offerings.

Are JILI and PG SOFT games identical?

No. They are different game providers. Individual titles can differ in interface, mechanics, published rules and availability.

Does RTP predict the next result?

No. RTP describes a theoretical long-run return under specified conditions and does not predict individual outcomes.

Where can I find current game information?

Visit the BingoPlus Slot information website and consult each game's published information.

Responsible play

For eligible adults only

Gaming involving wagering is intended for eligible adults aged 21 and above in the Philippines, subject to applicable rules. Gambling carries financial risk and should not be treated as income. Know when to stop.
